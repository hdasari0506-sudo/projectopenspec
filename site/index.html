Reading the whole dog

Happy & relaxed

A comfortable dog often looks soft and loose from nose to tail. Check the full picture and what is happening around them.

TailLoose, easy wag or resting naturally.
EarsNeutral or gently perked, not pinned tight.
EyesSoft gaze with relaxed facial muscles.
PostureLoose body, easy movement, weight balanced.
💡Try this: Let your dog choose whether to approach; reward calm check-ins and give them room to rest.
